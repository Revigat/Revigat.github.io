THE PROBLEM

The ad delivers. The page throws the lead away.

You swap the creative, the budget, and the audience. The landing stays heavy and slow on the phone and on the computer. The algorithm only delivers the click. The page is what loses the person.

A bloated page

A heavy library for a job the browser already does. The person waits and leaves before the offer.

The button disappears

A popup on load, a sticky bar over the CTA, a tiny tap target. They want to buy and cannot reach it.

The form kicks them out

The wrong phone keyboard, a silent error, a stuck checkout. The lead arrives and the page sends it back.
